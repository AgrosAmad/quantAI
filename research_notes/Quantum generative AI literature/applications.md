# Applications of quantum generative models across domains (2019 to November 2026)

Scope: application papers and hardware demonstrations of quantum GANs, quantum circuit Born machines (QCBMs), quantum diffusion models, quantum VAEs, quantum Boltzmann machines (QBMs) and hybrid quantum-classical generative pipelines, organised by application domain. Compiled 2026-11-03.

Verification note. Every arXiv ID below was resolved on 2026-11-03 against the arXiv DataCite/OpenAlex records and the returned title was checked against the paper named here (the arXiv and Semantic Scholar APIs rate-limited this machine, so DataCite/OpenAlex were used; zero IDs failed to resolve). Non-arXiv DOIs were resolved through Crossref and doi.org. One ID recalled from memory (2210.16057 for Chang et al. 2022) turned out to point to an unrelated paper and was replaced by the verified 2205.15003. Journal venues are given where I know them; where a journal version could not be confirmed the venue column says "arXiv preprint". Two papers already in the local reference folder are included: ORCA's arXiv:2508.19857 (R1) and Xiao et al. 2024 (R2, DOI 10.1038/s42005-024-01765-9).

Reading guide for the priorities: "must-read" means the paper is either a canonical reference for its domain, a genuine hardware demonstration with a classical comparison, or directly relevant to fluids/PDE simulation; "useful" means a solid application paper worth citing; "peripheral" means context, survey, theory, or only loosely generative.

## 1. Image generation (MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, CIFAR, medical imaging, super-resolution)

### Takeaway
Image generation is the most crowded application area, but almost everything beyond toy resolution is achieved by putting a small quantum model in a latent space or at the input of a classical generator (QCBM priors, latent diffusion, latent GANs). Direct quantum generation remains at MNIST scale; the medical-imaging papers of 2024-2025 are the first to report externally validated quality gains, and only two hardware image-generation results of 2025 use more than a handful of qubits.

### Cited Findings
- First experimental quantum GAN for image generation ran on a superconducting processor and, per the authors, matched classical GAN-MLP and GAN-CNN baselines with a similar number of parameters on small handwritten-digit images — [Huang et al. 2021, arXiv:2010.06201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06201)
- A QCBM prior trained on an ion-trap quantum computer (IonQ) fed a classical generator to produce 28x28 handwritten digits; the authors describe it as the first practical quantum-assisted generation of high-resolution images on gate-based hardware — [Rudolph et al. 2022, arXiv:2012.03924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03924)
- The hybrid patch-based PQWGAN generated 28x28 grey-scale MNIST and Fashion-MNIST images without dimensionality reduction, in simulation — [Tsang et al. 2023, arXiv:2212.11614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11614)
- MosaiQ is a quantum GAN framework for MNIST-class images designed to execute on today's NISQ machines, addressing the poor quality and robustness of earlier approaches — [Silver et al. 2023, arXiv:2308.11096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11096)
- LaSt-QGAN (CERN) trains a hybrid GAN in the latent space of a classical autoencoder to generate "large-size images comparable to classical counterparts" — [Chang et al. 2024, arXiv:2406.02668](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02668)
- Quantum denoising diffusion models were benchmarked on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 with reported gains over classical models of similar parameter count, plus a one-step consistency-style variant — [Kölle et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.07049](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07049)
- Quantum latent diffusion: a classical autoencoder compresses images and variational circuits perform diffusion in the latent space, compared against a classical latent diffusion model — [De Falco et al. 2025, arXiv:2501.11174](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11174); earlier hybrid-diffusion study — [De Falco et al. 2024, arXiv:2402.16147](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16147)
- Two physics-inspired diffusion protocols: quantum stochastic walks in the forward process gave MNIST generators with lower FID than fully classical dynamics, and a 4-qubit image generator was run on IBM's ibm_brisbane using hardware noise as a resource — [Parigi et al. 2025, arXiv:2505.22193](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22193); the earlier quantum-noise-driven diffusion proposal is [Parigi, Martina, Caruso 2023, arXiv:2308.12013](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12013)
- Quantum-annealer-assisted deep generative models: D-Wave 2000Q used as the Boltzmann sampler of a QVAE on MNIST — [Vinci et al. 2020, arXiv:1912.02119](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.02119); a D-Wave-sampled Boltzmann machine replacing the GAN noise input on binarized MNIST — [Wilson et al. 2021, arXiv:1904.10573](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10573); a D-Wave RBM inside a convolutional autoencoder on MNIST/Fashion-MNIST evaluated via downstream classification on generated samples — [Sleeman et al. 2020, arXiv:2001.11946](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11946)
- Medical: QIGL uses a quantum generator with PCA-reduced inputs and a Wasserstein loss on knee osteoarthritis X-rays and MedMNIST — [Khatun et al. 2024, arXiv:2406.13196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13196); a hybrid quantum-classical latent diffusion model and quantum VAE for fundus retinal images produced images of which 86% were rated gradable by external validation versus 69% for the classical model, with noisy-simulation and hardware tests — [Yeter-Aydeniz et al. 2025, arXiv:2508.09903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09903); MediQ-GAN (quantum-inspired, not run on QPUs) reports beating GAN and diffusion baselines on ISIC 2019, fundus and RetinaMNIST — [Jiao et al. 2025, arXiv:2506.21015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21015)
- Super-resolution: QUIET-SR adds shifted quantum window attention to a Swin transformer with fewer than 10 qubits per circuit, reporting 30.24 dB PSNR / 0.989 SSIM on MNIST and 29.76 / 0.976 on Fashion-MNIST — [Dutta et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.08759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08759)
- Inverse imaging: a programmable-quantum-circuit latent code inside a pretrained deep generative prior improved optical ghost-imaging reconstruction at low sampling rates and outperformed classical counterparts on inpainting and colorization "by a significant margin" (authors' claim) — [Xiao et al. 2024, DOI 10.1038/s42005-024-01765-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-024-01765-9)
- Materials imaging on hardware: a quantum circuit at the input layer of a large classical Wasserstein GAN generated 5-channel EBSD images of two steel microstructure phases end-to-end on an IonQ trapped-ion QPU, mitigating mode collapse and (per the authors) improving image quality for data augmentation — [Sekwao et al. 2025, arXiv:2504.08728](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08728)
- Earth observation: QCU-Net puts quanvolutional layers into a conditional diffusion U-Net to synthesise labelled satellite imagery and is reported to beat classical diffusion baselines (journal version IEEE TGRS 2026) — [Mauro et al. 2025, arXiv:2512.20448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20448)

### Inferences
- Every result at 28x28 or above relies on a classical autoencoder, PCA, patching or a classical generator; the quantum part is a prior or a latent denoiser of a few qubits. Claims of "beyond toy resolution" should be read as "toy-size quantum model inside a classical pipeline".
- Hardware image generation in 2025 is still 4 qubits (Parigi) or a quantum input layer (IonQ steel); the medical fundus result is the first with an external clinical-style quality metric.

### Gaps
- No verified quantum generative result on CIFAR-10 beyond Kölle et al. (simulation); no hardware CIFAR result found.
- No verified paper demonstrating a quantum GAN on CelebA or higher-resolution natural images without classical latent compression.

## 2. Molecules, drug discovery and materials

### Takeaway
This is the domain with the strongest "end-to-end" stories: a 16-qubit IBM-trained hybrid generative model produced synthesised and experimentally tested KRAS inhibitor candidates (Nature Biotechnology 2025), D-Wave annealer-sampled Boltzmann priors improved molecular validity from 73% to 97% in a Scientific Reports 2026 study with Shionogi, and ORCA's photonic latent distributions improved QM9 generation. QM9 is the standard benchmark (ZINC did not appear in any verified paper); claimed gains are typically in validity, QED or a drug-candidate score, not in absolute chemistry.

### Cited Findings
- Qubit-efficient hybrid QGAN (QGAN-HG) for QM9-like small molecules; the authors note a fully quantum GAN would need more than 90 qubits even for QM9 — [Li, Topaloglu, Ghosh 2021, arXiv:2101.03438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.03438)
- Quantum GAN variants of MolGAN on QM9 explored for lead-candidate discovery (JCIM 2023) — [Kao et al. 2023, arXiv:2210.16823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.16823)
- Hybrid quantum-classical generative model whose quantum component was trained on a 16-qubit IBM quantum computer designed KRAS inhibitors; 15 molecules were synthesised and experimentally evaluated (Insilico Medicine, University of Toronto, Zapata AI, St. Jude and others; Nature Biotechnology 2025) — [Ghazi Vakili et al., arXiv:2402.08210](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08210), journal DOI [10.1038/s41587-024-02526-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-024-02526-3)
- Zapata's quantum-inspired tensor-network Born machines applied to a 4,989-molecule QM9 subset and a 516-molecule in-house dataset — [Moussa et al. 2023, arXiv:2304.10867](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.10867)
- Terra Quantum's hybrid quantum cycle MolGAN on QM9 and PC9 reports up to 30% higher QED than prior scores — [Anoshin et al. 2024, arXiv:2402.00014](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00014)
- A compact discrete VAE with an RBM latent small enough for a D-Wave annealer was trained on a ChEMBL subset and generated 2,331 novel structures (Scientific Reports 2023) — [Gircha et al., arXiv:2108.11644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.11644)
- Multi-objective Bayesian optimisation of the quantum-classical bridge in molecular GANs (BO-QGAN): 2.27x higher Drug Candidate Score than prior quantum-hybrid benchmarks and 2.21x higher than the classical baseline with more than 60% fewer parameters; best designs stack 3-4 shallow 4-8-qubit circuits — [Smith et al. 2025, arXiv:2506.01177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01177)
- QCA-MolGAN uses a QCBM as a learnable latent prior associatively trained against the discriminator, plus multi-agent RL for property targeting, on a QM9 5k subset — [Thomas et al. 2025, arXiv:2509.05051](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05051)
- Patched parameterised-quantum-circuit GAN on QM9 (Fractal Analytics) — [Jain et al. 2022, arXiv:2212.07826](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07826)
- D-Wave Advantage2 sampled the Boltzmann-machine prior of a transformer molecular generator (Shionogi, formerly Japan Tobacco's pharma division): 97% chemically valid molecules versus 73% for the same architecture with classical MCMC sampling and 54% for a published benchmark; QED >= 0.7 share 66.79% versus 43.15% (best classical) and 31.61% in the training data — [Kunugi et al. 2026, arXiv:2602.15451](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15451), Scientific Reports DOI [10.1038/s41598-026-49186-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-49186-8); D-Wave's account of the study — [D-Wave blog, 2026-08-20](https://www.dwavequantum.com/learn/blog/posts/how-quantum-computing-could-improve-generative-ai-what-a-new-drug-discovery-study-reveals/)
- ORCA: quantum latent distributions from a PT-2 photonic processor (up to 16 photons in 32 time bins, hosted at the UK NQCC) improved generative performance over classical latents on a synthetic quantum dataset and on QM9, with a theory of when quantum latents enlarge the reachable data distributions — [Bacarreza et al. 2025 (v3 June 2026), arXiv:2508.19857](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19857)
- Peptides: a GAN with latent vectors sampled from a real photonic quantum processor (ORCA, Sparrow Quantum, DTU) increased the in-silico yield of predicted strong MHC-I binders across 131 HLA alleles, with in-vitro peptide-MHC stability ELISAs for three alleles; bioRxiv, not peer reviewed — [Engdal et al. 2026, bioRxiv DOI 10.64898/2026.07.09.736951](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.09.736951); ORCA's earlier announcement gave no numbers — [ORCA blog, 2024-10-10](https://orcacomputing.com/quantum-enhanced-vaccine-design-on-the-orca-pt-series/)
- Generative models that emit quantum circuits for chemistry: the generative quantum eigensolver (GPT-QE) surpassed CCSD on N2 bond dissociation in simulation — [Nakaji et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.09253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09253); ADAPT-GQE (Quantinuum, NVIDIA, Pfizer) trained transformers on ADAPT-VQE circuits and executed imipramine ground-state circuits on Quantinuum's Helios — [Koziell-Pipe et al. 2026, arXiv:2607.22468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22468); Quantinuum reports circuit generation about one order of magnitude faster than ADAPT-VQE and calls it a proof of principle — [Quantinuum blog, 2026-07-29](https://www.quantinuum.com/blog/quantinuum-and-nvidia-validate-generative-quantum-ai-framework-for-pharmaceutical-r-d); trade press quotes 3-4 orders of magnitude for 12-16 qubit systems, so the two accounts conflict — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-nvidia-and-pfizer-validate-generative-quantum-ai-genqai-framework-for-pharmaceutical-rd/)
- Steel microstructure EBSD image augmentation on IonQ hardware (see Section 1) is the only verified materials-science generative hardware demonstration — [Sekwao et al. 2025, arXiv:2504.08728](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08728)
- A physics-based (non-generative) quantum pipeline for de-novo protein-binding peptides, explicitly contrasted with generative models — [Meuser et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.05458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05458)

### Inferences
- The two most-cited "quantum wins" (KRAS, Shionogi) both use the quantum device only as a sampler for a small prior (QCBM or Boltzmann machine) inside a large classical generator; the comparison is against the same architecture with a classical prior, not against the best classical drug-design model.
- QM9 (or small subsets) is the de-facto benchmark; property metrics (QED, SA, LogP, validity) dominate; no paper reports docking or wet-lab results except KRAS and the peptide preprint.

### Gaps
- No verified paper applying a quantum generative model to crystal-structure or periodic-materials generation; the only lead is an APS March Meeting 2026 abstract on a hybrid quantum transformer with SLICES representations (no preprint found).
- ZINC was not used in any verified paper in this catalogue.
- The 2025 Japan Tobacco/D-Wave press release (see Section 8) has no accompanying paper; the Shionogi paper is the peer-reviewed follow-on.

## 3. Finance

### Takeaway
Finance was the first "real data" test bed for QCBMs and qGANs (distribution loading for option pricing, FX and equity copulas), with hardware runs on IBM, Rigetti and IonQ as early as 2019-2022; the consistent finding is parity or small gains on tiny (2-4 variable, 8-12 qubit) problems. The 2025-2026 work moves to time-series with temporal correlations, LLM-designed hardware-aware ansätze and tabular synthetic data on 100+ qubit IBM devices.

### Cited Findings
- qGANs learn and load random distributions (e.g., log-normal) for amplitude-estimation pricing, tested on IBM Q hardware (npj QI 2019) — [Zoufal, Lucchi, Woerner 2019, arXiv:1904.00043](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.00043)
- "The Market Generator": a QCBM trained on financial time series (Standard Chartered) — [Kondratyev and Schwarz 2019, SSRN DOI 10.2139/ssrn.3384948](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.3384948); gradient-free genetic-algorithm training of the QCBM (Wilmott 2021, paywalled) — [Kondratyev 2021, DOI 10.1002/wilm.10943](https://doi.org/10.1002/wilm.10943)
- QCBM versus RBM on real-world stock-market data (MLST 2020) — [Alcazar, Leyton-Ortega, Perdomo-Ortiz 2020, arXiv:1908.10778](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.10778)
- QCBM versus RBM on correlated currency pairs: the simulated Born machine always at least matched the RBM, with hardware runs on Rigetti (QST 2021) — [Coyle et al. 2021, arXiv:2008.00691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.00691)
- Copulas mapped to maximally entangled states ("qopula"); QGAN and QCBM trained on two-variable joint distributions of historical stock data on IonQ trapped-ion hardware with up to 8 qubits, reported to outperform equivalent classical generative methods (PRR 2022) — [Zhu et al. 2022, arXiv:2109.06315](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06315)
- IonQ and GE Research: QCBM copulas with 3-4 variables trained on IonQ hardware with four stock indexes, "comparable or better" risk-aggregation predictions than standard classical models — [Zhu (Daiwei) et al. 2022, arXiv:2206.11937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11937); press release — [BusinessWire, 2022-06-23](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220623005345/en/IonQ-and-GE-Research-Demonstrate-High-Potential-of-Quantum-Computing-for-Risk-Aggregation)
- Fully connected quantum GAN applied to volatility modelling — [Assouel, Jacquier, Kondratyev 2022, arXiv:2110.02742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02742)
- Generator-enhanced optimisation (GEO, Zapata): tensor-network Born machines improve classical solvers on portfolio optimisation (Nature Communications 2024) — [Alcazar et al., arXiv:2101.06250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06250), DOI [10.1038/s41467-024-46959-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46959-5)
- Anomaly detection with variational quantum-classical Wasserstein GANs (fraud-style tabular data; QST 2021) — [Herr, Obert, Rosenkranz 2021, arXiv:2010.10492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10492)
- Quantum Wasserstein GAN synthetic S&P 500 data to improve time-series prediction (Electronics 2024, open access) — [Orlandi et al. 2024, DOI 10.3390/electronics13112158](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics13112158)
- QGANs for financial time series with temporal correlations, simulated exactly and with MPS tensor networks to reach longer series (MLST 7:015027, 2026) — [Dechant et al. 2025, arXiv:2507.22035](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22035)
- LLM-generated hardware-aware QCBM ansätze for daily changes in Japanese government bond rates were significantly shallower and generatively better than the standard baseline when executed on real IBM hardware with 12 qubits — [Gujju et al. 2025, arXiv:2509.08385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08385)
- Tabular synthetic data: TabularQGAN (SAP, BASF Digital Solutions, QUTAC) with flexible encoding for mixed categorical/numerical features — [Bhardwaj et al. 2025, arXiv:2505.22533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22533); QTabGAN claims up to 54.07% improvement on classification datasets — [Kumari et al. 2026, arXiv:2602.12704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12704)
- In-silico clinical trial patients from scarce data: a quantum generative circuit evaluated on IBM's 156-qubit Heron r2 device ibm_basquecountry achieved better MAE and Jensen-Shannon fidelity than TVAE, CTGAN and CopulaGAN across sample sizes (authors' claim) — [Sanz Larrarte et al. 2026, arXiv:2608.28168](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28168)

### Inferences
- Where hardware is used, the quantum model handles 2-4 correlated variables; the demonstrated value is in heavy-tailed or strongly non-Gaussian dependence (copulas), which is also exactly where IonQ's 2026 SAR work (Section 5) reports gains.

### Gaps
- No JPMorgan or Goldman Sachs quantum generative-modelling paper surfaced in searches; their published quantum-finance work is on amplitude estimation and derivative pricing (not catalogued here).
- No independent replication of the hardware finance results.

## 4. High-energy physics (calorimeter showers, event generation, jets, anomaly detection)

### Takeaway
CERN openlab/QTI, DESY and ORNL produced a coherent body of work from 2021 to 2025: qGANs and conditional Born machines for Monte Carlo event generation and downsized calorimeter images, with runs on IBM and trapped-ion hardware; quantum-annealer-assisted VAEs (CaloQVAE) are the only approach touching realistic calorimeter dimensionality. Performance is "comparable" to small classical models; no paper claims a speed or accuracy advantage over production fast-simulation tools.

### Cited Findings
- Style-based qGAN for LHC Monte Carlo events reached smaller KL divergence than earlier architectures even at shallow depth, learned from small training sets, and was deployed on both trapped-ion and superconducting hardware (Quantum 2022) — [Bravo-Prieto et al., arXiv:2110.06933](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06933)
- Conditional Born machine for multivariate, conditional Monte Carlo generation of muonic-force-carrier events, run on noisy simulators and IBM Quantum hardware (PRA 2022) — [Kiss et al. 2022, arXiv:2205.07674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07674)
- Dual-PQC GAN: two quantum generators (pixel distribution and intensities) imitating calorimeter outputs (CHEP 2021 proceedings) — [Chang et al. 2021, arXiv:2103.15470](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.15470); run under two-qubit gate and readout noise and then on superconducting and trapped-ion hardware (J. Phys. Conf. Ser. 2438, 012062) — [Chang et al. 2022, arXiv:2205.15003](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15003); continuous-variable qGAN prototype for detector simulation — [Chang et al. 2021, arXiv:2101.11132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.11132)
- Impact of readout and two-qubit errors on qGAN training thresholds (DESY/CERN) — [Borras et al. 2023, arXiv:2203.01007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01007)
- Full qGAN (quantum generator and discriminator) for eight-pixel calorimeter images; authors note that converging full qGANs remain rare — [Rehm et al. 2023, arXiv:2305.07284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07284)
- Quantum Angle Generator with MERA upsampling: the model trained on a physical quantum device learns the device noise characteristics; authors call its accuracy a first for a quantum model (QST 2024) — [Rehm et al. 2023, arXiv:2307.05253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.05253)
- Non-adversarial QCBM training for 2- and 3-variable joint distributions of LHC di-jet kinematics (PRD 2022) — [Delgado and Hamilton 2022, arXiv:2203.03578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03578)
- A hybrid quantum invertible neural network matched a significantly larger classical INN on Z+jet data (SciPost Physics 2024) — [Rousselot and Spannowsky, arXiv:2302.12906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12906)
- qGANs for unsupervised anomaly detection of beyond-Standard-Model events (IBM Zurich and CERN) — [Bermot et al. 2023, arXiv:2304.14439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14439)
- qGAN-loaded distributions integrated with quantum amplitude estimation for e+e- processes, per-cent accuracy with up to six qubits in noiseless simulation (PLB 2022) — [Agliardi et al. 2022, arXiv:2201.01547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01547)
- CaloQVAE: quantum-annealing-assisted VAE for calorimeter showers — [Hoque et al. 2024, arXiv:2312.03179](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03179); conditioned follow-up published in npj Quantum Information 2025 — [Toledo-Marín et al., arXiv:2410.22870](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22870), DOI [10.1038/s41534-025-01040-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-025-01040-x)
- Quantum diffusion model for quark/gluon jets with random unitaries as forward noise and a VQC inside the U-Net, competitive with a similar classical model — [Baidachna et al. 2024, arXiv:2412.21082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.21082)
- qGAN generating ECAL and HCAL gluon-jet images from CMS Open Data, limited to 512 downscaled images on simulators — [Guadarrama et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.05044](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05044)

### Inferences
- Realistic shower dimensionality is only reached through annealer-sampled latent priors (CaloQVAE); gate-model qGANs are limited to 8-pixel or few-variable targets, which is why the CERN group moved to latent-space GANs (LaSt-QGAN, Section 1).

### Gaps
- 2025-2026 ATLAS and IHEP "quantum FastCaloGAN" work exists only as conference talks (CHEP/ISGC indico pages); no preprint was found.

## 5. Physics and engineering simulation (fluids, turbulence, PDE surrogates, climate, inverse problems)

### Takeaway
This niche is small but has three genuine 2025-2026 fluid-dynamics papers: a quantum-circuit sampler for Lagrangian tracer velocities in turbulent shear flow run on IQM hardware (Physics of Fluids 2025), a QCBM/QGAN comparison on VQ-VAE latents of lattice-Boltzmann vorticity fields, and a Science Advances 2026 paper where a hardware-trained "quantum prior" stabilises long-horizon forecasts of Kolmogorov and channel flow. Weather/climate has two hybrid diffusion papers (downscaling, Earth observation) and a Rigetti synthetic-radar study. No quantum generative PDE surrogate or SPH-related work exists.

### Cited Findings
- Quantum-assisted tracer dispersion: a parametric quantum circuit encodes the joint PDF of three velocity-fluctuation components in homogeneous turbulent shear flow; samples come from one-shot measurements; validated against a stochastic Lagrangian model and an MCMC sampler; hardware runs on the 20-qubit IQM Resonance platform with up to 10 qubits (Physics of Fluids 37, 085251, 2025) — [Ingelmann, Schindler, Schumacher 2025, arXiv:2506.14586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14586), DOI [10.1063/5.0285751](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0285751)
- Quantum generative models for CFD: GPU lattice-Boltzmann vorticity fields compressed to a discrete 7-dimensional VQ-VAE latent; a QCBM and a QGAN produced samples with lower average minimum distance to the true latent distribution than an LSTM baseline, QCBM best; no hardware, exploratory — [Hsain and Abbou 2025, arXiv:2512.22672](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22672)
- Quantum-informed ML for spatiotemporal chaos: an offline-trained quantum generative model supplies a "Q-Prior" to a classical autoregressive predictor on Kuramoto-Sivashinsky, 2D Kolmogorov flow and 3D turbulent channel inflow; up to 17.25% better predictive-distribution accuracy and 29.36% better full-spectrum fidelity; the channel-inflow Q-Prior was trained on a superconducting quantum processor and the classical model without it became unstable (Science Advances 12(16), 2026) — [Wang, Xue, Gao, Coveney, arXiv:2507.19861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19861)
- Hybrid quantum-classical corrective diffusion for meteorological downscaling of 10 m wind: VQC layers at the U-Net bottleneck improved MAE and CRPS in several configurations on the 2020 validation set, preserved kinetic-energy spectra, but gains did not transfer uniformly to the 2021 out-of-distribution year; simulated device noise was negligible at the tested scale (submitted to IEEE QCE 2026) — [Wang et al. 2026, arXiv:2605.23403](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23403)
- Rigetti: quantum-assisted models augmenting CNNs for global synthetic weather radar, with a quantum-kernel argument for why the task could be a quantum-advantage benchmark (NeurIPS 2021 workshop) — [Enos et al. 2021, arXiv:2111.15605](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.15605)
- Review of quantum computing for climate modelling that lists quantum generative models as candidates for learning the distributions behind climate-model output — [Schwabe et al. 2025, arXiv:2502.10488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10488)
- Quantum conditioned diffusion for Earth-observation data enrichment (QCU-Net; see Section 1) — [Mauro et al. 2025, arXiv:2512.20448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20448)
- IonQ and Capella Space: a QCBM-sampled generative model in copula space replaced the empirical background estimator for SAR/InSAR change detection; training and inference ran on an IonQ Forte Enterprise QPU with 20-qubit circuits; on the sparse, heavy-tailed Miramar airfield SAR set the QPU reached filtered F1 0.32 (0.37 in one configuration) versus 0.16 and 0.24 for two classical baselines, while on Piton de la Fournaise InSAR lava flows quantum and classical were comparable (about 0.66) and the gap vanished on Gaussianised data — [Sekwao et al. 2026, arXiv:2609.05313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05313); IonQ's press release quotes 0.41 for Miramar, higher than the paper's abstract — [IonQ press release, 2026-09-24](https://www.ionq.com/news/ionq-demonstrates-quantum-generative-modeling-for-high-resolution-radar-change-detection)
- Differentiable quantum generative modelling (Pasqal/Exeter): phase-feature-map latent training mapped to the bit basis via a QFT for single-shot sampling, with samples differentiable for SDE and physics applications — [Kyriienko, Paine, Elfving 2022, arXiv:2202.08253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08253); quantum quantile mechanics solves SDEs (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck) to generate time series and shows continuous qGANs represent quantile functions — [Paine, Elfving, Kyriienko 2021, arXiv:2108.03190](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.03190); classical training protocols for these models — [Kasture, Kyriienko, Elfving 2022, arXiv:2210.13442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13442); Chebyshev feature maps for distribution learning and sampling — [Williams et al. 2023, arXiv:2306.17026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17026)
- Quantum-enhanced MCMC samples Boltzmann distributions of classical Ising models on a superconducting processor (Nature 2023), relevant as a quantum sampler for statistical-physics generative tasks — [Layden et al., arXiv:2203.12497](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.12497)
- Inverse problems: QEKI pairs quantum neural-network surrogates with ensemble Kalman inversion for Bayesian PDE inversion (surrogate, not generative; Entropy 2026) — [Yong et al. 2026, DOI 10.3390/e28020156](https://doi.org/10.3390/e28020156); quantum model discovery for differential equations — [Heim et al. 2021, arXiv:2111.06376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06376); the only generative-prior inverse-problem paper is imaging-based (ghost imaging) — [Xiao et al. 2024, DOI 10.1038/s42005-024-01765-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-024-01765-9)
- Non-generative but adjacent turbulence QML: hybrid quantum POD plus quantum deep kernel learning reduced-order model of turbulent flows — [Li et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.18552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18552)

### Inferences
- For an SPH/Lagrangian practitioner the closest entry point is Ingelmann et al. (quantum circuit as a sampler of velocity-increment PDFs for Lagrangian tracers) followed by Wang et al. (quantum prior for inflow turbulence); both treat the quantum device as a learned distribution sampler, which maps naturally onto stochastic subgrid or particle-closure models.
- The CFD latent-space paper is methodologically the same recipe as the image and HEP latent-GAN work (classical compression plus a few-qubit sampler); its baseline (an LSTM) is weak, so the claimed gain is not strong evidence.

### Gaps
- No paper combining a quantum generative model with SPH, particle methods or mesh-free simulation beyond Lagrangian tracer statistics.
- No quantum generative PDE surrogate (neural-operator style) or quantum diffusion-based flow super-resolution; the quantum-airfoil-GAN query returned only classical work.
- Qubit counts and hardware are unstated in the CFD latent paper; the downscaling paper's spatial resolution and data source are not stated in the abstract page.

## 6. Quantum-physics use cases (state preparation, learning state ensembles, QEC decoders, ground states)

### Takeaway
Here "generative" splits into (a) quantum models generating quantum data (QuDDPM, ensemble learning, EQ-GAN on Sycamore), (b) classical generative models applied to quantum problems (state tomography with RNN/transformers, generative QEC decoders, GQE for circuits), and (c) QBM theory with ground-state and thermal-state framing. Google's 2025 68-qubit "generative quantum advantage" paper is the headline result.

### Cited Findings
- Google Quantum AI: families of generative quantum models that are classically hard to simulate yet efficiently trainable without barren plateaus, demonstrated on a 68-qubit superconducting processor for learning classically intractable distributions and for learning circuits to accelerate physical simulation — [Huang et al. 2025, arXiv:2509.09033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09033)
- QuDDPM: quantum denoising diffusion for quantum data (correlated noise, many-body phases, topological structure; PRL 132, 100602) — [Zhang et al. 2024, arXiv:2310.05866](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05866); mixed-state variant removing scrambling unitaries — [Kwun, Zhang, Zhuang 2024, arXiv:2411.17608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17608); fully quantum-channel diffusion for state ensembles — [Chen et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.07039](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07039)
- Learning a quantum state ensemble with an optimal-transport loss (application: anomaly detection; QMI 2024) — [Tezuka, Uno, Yamamoto 2022, arXiv:2210.10743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10743); universality of the many-body projected ensemble for learning pure-state distributions within 1-Wasserstein error (Fujitsu Research, per press coverage) — [Tran et al. 2026, arXiv:2601.18637](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18637)
- Hardware quantum adversarial learning: superconducting circuit (Science Advances 2019) — [Hu et al., arXiv:1808.02893](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.02893); EQ-GAN with convergence guarantees demonstrated on Google hardware (PRL 2022) — [Niu et al., arXiv:2105.00080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.00080); hybrid QGAN for continuous distributions with a noise study and hardware demonstration — [Anand et al. 2021, arXiv:2006.01976](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01976); adversarial pure-state approximation (NJP 2019) — [Benedetti et al., arXiv:1806.00463](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00463)
- Classical generative models for state reconstruction (Nature Machine Intelligence 2019) — [Carrasquilla et al., arXiv:1810.10584](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10584); complex-valued basis-enhanced Born machine reconstructs 1D Rydberg-chain ground states with fidelity above 99% from two Pauli bases — [McClain Gomez et al. 2022, arXiv:2206.01273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.01273); RydbergGPT autoregressive model of 2D Rydberg arrays — [Fitzek et al. 2024, arXiv:2405.21052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21052)
- QBMs: variational QBMs with hidden units, Gibbs states via imaginary-time evolution, with IBM hardware experiments (QMI 2021) — [Zoufal, Lucchi, Woerner, arXiv:2006.06004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06004); Born machines as variational posteriors for Bayesian inference (PRApplied 2021) — [Benedetti et al., arXiv:2103.06720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06720); sample complexity of QBM learning (Communications Physics 2024) — [Coopmans and Benedetti, arXiv:2306.14969](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14969); QBM learning of ground-state energies — [Patel et al. 2024, arXiv:2410.12935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12935); low-dimensional QBMs generating high-dimensional distributions — [Tüysüz et al. 2024, arXiv:2410.16363](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16363); semi-quantum RBMs need about one third of the hidden units of a classical RBM (Communications Physics 2025, simulations up to 100 units) — [Demidik et al. 2025, arXiv:2502.17562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17562); evolved QBMs — [Minervini et al. 2025, arXiv:2501.03367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03367) and [Wilde 2025, arXiv:2512.02721](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02721); practical obstacles implementing a QBM on NISQ hardware — [Paul et al. 2023, arXiv:2301.13705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13705)
- QEC decoders framed as generative modelling (classical): qecGPT pre-trains causal transformers without labels — [Cao et al. 2023, arXiv:2307.09025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09025); generative neural decoder reported more accurate than MWPM and BP-OSD on surface and qLDPC codes — [Cao et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.21374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21374); a GAN decoder on the toric code roughly doubled the pseudo-threshold (0.2108 vs 0.1099) in simulation — [Li et al. 2024, arXiv:2409.06984](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06984)
- Ground states via generative circuit synthesis: GQE/GPT-QE — [Nakaji et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.09253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09253) and ADAPT-GQE on Helios — [Koziell-Pipe et al. 2026, arXiv:2607.22468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22468)

### Inferences
- The QEC and GQE lines are classical generative AI serving quantum computing, not quantum generative models; they are included because the user asked for "ground states where framed as generative modelling", and because they are where "generative" and "quantum" currently meet hardware (Helios, Sycamore-class processors).

### Gaps
- No hardware demonstration of a trained quantum Boltzmann machine beyond the small VQBM IBM experiments and annealer-based classical-BM sampling (Vinci, Kunugi) was found for 2025-2026.

## 7. Other domains (anomaly detection, cybersecurity, graphs, text, audio, tabular/privacy, recommendation)

### Takeaway
Outside the core domains the literature is thin and mostly 2025-2026 preprints; graph generation has the largest hardware demonstration of any quantum generative model (IQP circuits sampled on 153 IBM qubits), cybersecurity has several QGAN-for-IDS papers with parity-level results, and music, recommendation and differential privacy have no verified quantum-GAN papers at all.

### Cited Findings
- Graphs: shallow IQP circuits trained classically and sampled on IBM's Aachen QPU from 28 to 153 qubits generate bipartite and Erdos-Renyi graphs of 8-18 nodes — [Balló-Gimbernat et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.05267](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05267); topology-guided hybrid QuGANs for constrained K4 graphs, best variant matching a classical GAN — [Rohe et al. 2025, arXiv:2512.10582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10582)

- Surveys: QNNs in generative learning — [Tian et al. 2022, arXiv:2206.03066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.03066); QGAN architectures, use cases and real-world implementations — [Islam et al. 2025, arXiv:2506.18002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18002)

### Gaps
- No verified quantum GAN or Born machine for music/audio synthesis, recommendation, or differentially private synthetic data (searches returned only classical DP-GANs and non-GAN quantum music systems).

## 9. Cross-cutting benchmarks and trainability (read before the application papers)

### Takeaway
The QCBM benchmarking papers (2018-2019) and the Zapata/Quantinuum generalisation and trainability papers (2022-2024) define how application claims should be judged: on generalisation to unseen valid samples, not on fitting the training distribution, and with awareness that explicit losses on deep circuits are untrainable.
